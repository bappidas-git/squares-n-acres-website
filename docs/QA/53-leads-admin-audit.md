# 53 — Leads admin audit (the CRM list, the lead detail and the leads API)

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Toolchain:** Node 22, Chromium (Playwright) ·
**Backend:** the mock on `:4000`, seeded from the committed `db.json` (45
leads), reset with `MOCK_FRESH=1` between passes.

Everything under **Leads** in the admin sidebar — the list at `/admin/leads`,
the detail at `/admin/leads/:id`, their dialogs, the bulk bar, the CSV export,
the "N new" chip and the badge and bell the lead poller drives — exercised as
admin, manager and sales at 1 920, 1 536 (a 1 920 screen at 125 %, where the
report that started this audit was taken), 1 440, 1 366, 1 280, 1 100, 1 024
and 390 px, with the fonts the panel falls back to and with a wider one, plus
every leads endpoint driven directly. 55 defects were found — 25 on the list,
16 on the detail and 14 in the API behind them — and all 55 are fixed.

Three of the API fixes change the contract the Laravel backend is built to, so
the notes, the contract and the handover package change with them (§4): dates
are IST days rather than UTC ones (D96 superseded), "Lost" needs a reason on the
API as it did in the dialog, and every timeline entry names its author.

## 3. Notes on three of the fixes

**Folding instead of scrolling.** The properties list (QA-51) answered the same
problem with the wide canvas, compact density and a sticky actions column; the
lead list gets all three, but at 1 280–1 535 px that still left one column too
many. The two that fold away — Source under the listing, priority under the
status — are pairs a reader asks about together, and each comes back as its own
column the moment there is room. Between 900 and 1 023 px with the sidebar
expanded the table still scrolls sideways behind the pinned actions and their
edge shadow; that range is narrower than any laptop, and collapsing the sidebar
removes it.

**One column that reads in order.** The detail's rail used to sit after the
whole record in the markup, so a phone reached the pipeline after the timeline.
It now follows the contact card, and on a wide screen a grid of
`'lead rail' / 'main rail'` with a flexible second row keeps the record under
the contact card however tall the rail is.

**The meta card and the seed.** Reading `meta` with the form's own labels needs
the forms' definitions, which `utils/leadSources` already holds for every
source; the eligibility check's live in `finance/financeCopy`. The seed's three
assessment leads carried values no form produces ("1l-2l", "750-800",
"20-percent"), so they now carry what `assessmentLead()` sends; `db.json` and
the handover package's copy were rebuilt from `scripts/seed/data/leads.js`.
